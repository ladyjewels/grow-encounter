Before We Begin

Welcome, Beloved

You did not arrive here by accident. Something in you said yes to this weekend before you understood why.

This workbook holds the six stages of your Encounter: Arrive, Reveal, Release, Restore, Renew, and Rise. Each stage gives you scripture to stand on and space to write what is true, not what sounds good.

Write honest. No one is grading these pages. Only you and God will read most of them.

Immersion02
Before We Begin

How This Workbook Works

Six stages. One question at a time. Answer in the moment you are asked, not after the session ends.

Bring a pen you like. Sit where you can hear yourself think. Turn the page when you are ready, not when the clock says to.

This book leaves the retreat with you. Keep it somewhere you will see it again.
